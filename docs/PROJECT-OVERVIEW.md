
# CloudVault - Project Overview

## 1. Introduction

CloudVault is a cross-platform cloud photo management
application that brings accessible photos and videos from
multiple connected cloud accounts into one interface.

Users can connect multiple Google accounts, browse
authorized media, organize collections and monitor
storage usage.

CloudVault does not own or provide unlimited cloud storage.
Original media remains with the respective cloud provider.

## 2. Problem Statement

Users often maintain multiple Google accounts.

Their photos and videos become distributed across different
accounts, making browsing and organization inconvenient.

Switching between accounts creates friction.

CloudVault aims to provide a centralized experience.

## 3. Product Vision

"All your memories. One place."

Create a secure, intuitive and reliable cloud photo
management experience across Android and iOS.

## 4. Target Users

- People with multiple Google accounts.
- Students and professionals.
- Users managing personal and work photographs.
- Users who want centralized cloud media browsing.

## 5. Core Value Proposition

- Multiple account management.
- Unified media browsing.
- Account-wise storage visibility.
- Centralized album organization.
- Secure cloud media access.
- Cross-platform mobile experience.

## 6. Supported Providers

### Google Drive
Access files according to the permissions granted
by the user.

### Google Photos
Allow users to select existing media through the
Google Photos Picker API.

These integrations have different permission models
and must remain separate in the architecture.

## 7. Product Principles

1. Privacy first.
2. Transparent permissions.
3. No misleading storage claims.
4. Fast gallery performance.
5. Simple user experience.
6. Reliable synchronization.
7. Cross-platform consistency.

## 8. Business Model - Future Consideration

Potential monetization:
- Free personal tier.
- Premium organization features.
- Advanced gallery management.
- Optional subscription features.

Do not introduce paid storage or claim to sell
Google storage.

## 9. Initial Product Scope

The MVP focuses on:
- User authentication.
- Multiple Google account connections.
- User-authorized Drive media.
- Unified gallery.
- Photo viewer.
- Storage dashboard.
- Account management.

Google Photos integration follows after the
Drive integration is validated.
